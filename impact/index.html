Impact report

There are no published figures yet, because the verification service is still in testing with made-up data. When real cases exist, this page will show counts with their denominators and the period covered, and any count below 10 will be hidden to protect students.

What we will report

  • Requests sent to universities and replies received.
  • Results by type: verified, contradicted, university declined, inconclusive, still awaiting.
  • Appeals filed and upheld, and how often people asked for human help.
  • The share of students who checked before paying, where they told us.

What the numbers will not show

  • Any individual student, school, university or city.
  • Whether a particular consultant is honest. We never publish accusations.

Last updated 14 October 2026

TaleemParakh is a free student-protection project, started and run by its founder. It does not confirm admissions, visas or scholarships: only the institution and the immigration authority can.
