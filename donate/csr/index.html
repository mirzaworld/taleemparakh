Corporate and CSR support

Companies can sponsor student protection programmes: school sessions, planned reviewer hours, staff training or infrastructure. Sponsorship never buys influence, exclusive access or the removal of any complaint, and every sponsor is listed publicly with any conflict of interest.

Ways to help

  • Sponsor sessions in schools and colleges.
  • Help pay for planned reviewer and safeguarding hours.
  • Offer staff training on spotting education scams.
  • Provide infrastructure or professional services free of charge.

Conditions for every sponsor

  • No influence over any finding, and no advance notice of any result.
  • No exclusive access to students, schools or data.
  • Conflicts of interest are published.
