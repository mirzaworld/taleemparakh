Sponsor a student's protection

Your pledge helps keep TaleemParakh free for every student. No money is taken today: we will contact you when we are able to accept donations lawfully. Donors receive anonymised reports, never student files, and can never influence any finding.

What the money will pay for

  • This is the plan. There is no paid team yet.
  • Money would pay for trained reviewers to check university replies before a student sees a result.
  • It would also pay for safeguarding: people who can respond when a student is threatened, distressed or about to pay.
  • And it would cover the small running costs of the service. Students never pay for anything.

What a donor never gets

  • Access to any student's case or documents.
  • Any say in a finding, a ranking, or which universities or consultants are checked.
  • Any personal benefit from a result.

Why only a pledge for now

  • TaleemParakh is not yet a registered organisation, so it cannot lawfully accept donations. Donations from outside Pakistan also need the right permissions first. A pledge tells us who wants to help so that we can contact you when this is in place.

We are not taking sign-ups yet. You can use every check, guide and news update now, for free.

To ask about supporting or working with TaleemParakh, email contact@taleemparakh.org.

Last updated 14 October 2026

TaleemParakh is a free student-protection project, started and run by its founder. It does not confirm admissions, visas or scholarships: only the institution and the immigration authority can.
