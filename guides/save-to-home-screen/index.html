Save TaleemParakh to your home screen

You can put TaleemParakh on your phone or laptop so it opens like an app. It is free. It does not use an app store, and it does not track you. Open TaleemParakh in the right browser, then use the browser's own menu to add it. The steps are below for iPhone, Android and laptop.

Before you start

Use the right browser. On an iPhone or iPad, use Safari. On an Android phone, use Chrome. On a laptop, use Chrome or Edge.

Check the address first. The real TaleemParakh address is the one in the box below. Copies of websites exist, and a copy can look the same. Type the address yourself or use a link from someone you trust, and do not use a link that came in an unexpected message.

The official address

mirzaworld.github.io/taleemparakh

iPhone and iPad (Safari)

Button names can look a little different on an older phone. If you cannot find a step, ask someone you trust to help you.

  1. Open TaleemParakh in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button. It is a square with an arrow pointing up.
  3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.
  4. Keep Open as Web App switched on. Apple's newer software (iOS 26 and later) opens a website added to the Home Screen as a web app by default.
  5. Tap Add. The TaleemParakh icon now sits on your Home Screen.

Android phone (Chrome)

If you do not see Install app, use the site for a little while and then open the menu again.

  1. Open TaleemParakh in Chrome.
  2. Tap the menu button with three dots at the top right.
  3. Tap Install app. If you see Add to home screen instead, tap that.
  4. Tap Install, or Add, and then confirm.
  5. Find the TaleemParakh icon on your home screen or in your apps.

Laptop (Chrome or Edge)

Edge usually offers the same thing in its own menu.

  1. Open TaleemParakh in Chrome or Edge.
  2. Look at the right end of the address bar for an install icon, and click it. In Chrome you can also open the menu, choose Cast, save, and share, and then Install page as app.
  3. Follow the instructions on the screen. TaleemParakh now opens in its own window.

What works without internet

The checks, the guides and the last news you opened work without internet. The register lists are the last copy your phone saved, and the app tells you when they may be out of date.

Sending or following a request needs internet. We never save your request to send later, so nothing is sent twice.

Signing in again

You may need to sign in again each time you open it. This is on purpose. It keeps your requests private if someone else uses your phone.

How updates arrive

When there is a new version, a bar says that a new version is ready. Tap Update now. You do not need an app store. Your saved requests stay in your account, not on the phone.

How to remove it

On a phone, press and hold the TaleemParakh icon until a menu opens, then choose the option to remove or delete it. This only removes the shortcut.

On a laptop in Chrome, open the app, open the menu at the top right and choose Uninstall TaleemParakh.

Staying safe

Saving TaleemParakh does not install anything that tracks you. It is the same website, kept as a shortcut. It has no ads and no trackers.

We never ask you to install any other app, and we never send links to install apps by WhatsApp or text message. If a message says it is from TaleemParakh and asks you to install something, it is not from us. Do not tap it.

If something looks wrong

Stop, do not enter any details, and tell a parent or someone you trust. The help numbers are at the end of this page.

Questions people ask

Does it cost money?

No. TaleemParakh is free, and saving it to your home screen is free.

Does it use an app store?

No. You add it from your browser. You never need an app store account.

Does it track me?

No. Saving it does not install anything that tracks you, and TaleemParakh has no ads or trackers.

Need help now?

TaleemParakh has no human reviewers yet and no crisis service. These are public services you can contact yourself. Please also talk to someone you trust today.

  • Emergency in Pakistan: Rescue 1122 (emergency services), Police 15. If you are thinking about hurting yourself or ending your life, call Rescue 1122 now, or go to the nearest hospital emergency department, or ask someone near you to take you. You can also tell an adult who is with you. You matter. You do not have to face this alone.
  • Feeling low or overwhelmed: Taskeen ⁦0316 8275336⁩ (free, Monday to Saturday, 11 am to 11 pm). Rozan Counseling Helpline ⁦0800‑22‑444⁩ (free, Monday to Friday, 9:30 am to 5 pm; in Ramadan 9:30 am to 3 pm). These are daytime lines, and Taskeen is not an emergency line.
  • Online fraud, fake messages or hacked accounts: National Cyber Crime Investigation Agency (NCCIA) helpline 1799, or complaint.nccia.gov.pk.
  • Fraud calls or SMS: report the number to your mobile company first, then to the Pakistan Telecommunication Authority (PTA) on ⁦0800‑55055⁩ or complaint.pta.gov.pk.
  • Money sent from a Pakistani bank or wallet: call your bank's helpline at once and ask them to stop the transfer. If the bank does not solve it, complain to the Banking Mohtasib (free).
  • In trouble abroad: the nearest Pakistani embassy, or the Foreign Office Crisis Management Unit, ⁦+92 51 9207887⁩ (a Foreign Office advisory of March 2026 described it as working 24 hours).
  • Human rights legal advice: Ministry of Human Rights 1099 (free, weekdays 8:30 am to 9 pm).
