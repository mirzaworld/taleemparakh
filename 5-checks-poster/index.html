5 checks before you pay or sell anything

For students applying to study abroad, and for their families. Free. Print this page.

  1. Ask the university yourself.

    Type the university's website address yourself. Write to its admissions office at the email address published there. Only the university can confirm an offer.

  2. Check the official register.

    Is the university on an official register? Use the free University tool in the Check tab. A listing shows only what the register says. It does not prove your offer.

  3. Check the email or link.

    Lookalike addresses and free email accounts are warning signs. Type the website address yourself, not from a link someone sent.

  4. Check where the money goes.

    Pay only through the payment page on the university's own website, to an account in the university's own name. Never to a person, a mobile wallet, crypto or gift cards.

  5. Stop and talk to someone you trust.

    Secrecy and pressure are the two strongest warning signs. Before you sell land, gold or a house, confirm the admission and the exact fee with the university first.
